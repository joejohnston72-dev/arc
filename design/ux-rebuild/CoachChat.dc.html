Hypertrophy · 5 days/wk · left shoulder impingement Edit
My left shoulder has been grumbling on incline pressing for about two weeks now. Should I drop the incline work entirely, or swap to something else? I don't want to lose the upper-chest stimulus.
Don't drop it — swap the angle. Your Smith incline is at 30°, which loads the anterior delt hard at the bottom. Two changes for the next four weeks: 1. Incline Bench (Smith) → Low-Incline Dumbbell Press at ~15°. Dumbbells let the humerus externally rotate, which is usually what settles impingement. 2. Cut the bottom third of the range for two sessions, then rebuild. Keep the volume the same — 4 × 5 at roughly 70% of your Smith load to start.
