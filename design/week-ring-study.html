The week ring

One element that is both the logo and a live part of the app. Your training week shows as a segmented ring at the top of Today. Complete the week and the ring fills in all four pillar colours, and that full ring is the app icon.

Icons B · Duotone Stroke 1.5 Tabs faint fill, full when selected 🏆 / 🔥 replaced by glyphs

How it connects

Logo variants

Four segments = four sessions, the default weekly target. Segments run clockwise from 12 o'clock, the same way every ring in the app fills. Shown at 150, 60 and 29px.

In the app

Today with the new ring in the date header, B icons at 1.5px and the flame and trophy glyphs in place of emoji. Mid-week the ring fills in Train blue; a complete week turns it into the logo.

Launch

The ring draws itself in on open instead of a blank load, so the first frame of the app is the home-screen icon.

Ring family

Every ring in the app shares one construction: round caps, a 10% white track, clockwise from 12 o'clock. Segmented rings count things; continuous rings measure amounts.

To decide

  1. Logo: Full week, Full week + A, Mist week or Week in motion
  2. Should the segment count follow the user's weekly target? (a target of 3 draws three segments in the app; the logo always stays at four)
  3. Week-complete moment: colour change only, or a short fill animation plus the trophy banner
  4. Launch animation: yes / no
