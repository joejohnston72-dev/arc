The four pillars
Train#9fb8ccWorkouts, routines, the split. The primary action colour. Shape: the one raised, tinted hero per screen — the only thing with depth.
Progress#8fc2bbStreaks, volume, PB trends, charts. Shape: no box. Big numbers sit on the canvas with sparklines and hairlines — data, not objects.
Fuel & body#d6b48eCalories, protein, bodyweight. Shape: a soft sand wash with rounded gauges — warm, gentle, no border.
Coach#b3aad6Anything the AI says. Shape: a lavender wash with a speech corner — reads as a voice, wherever it appears.
Rules that keep it calm
  • One raised thing per screen. Only the hero gets a gradient and shadow; everything else is flat.
  • Colour marks, never floods. Pillar hue goes on the label, icon, data line and the hero tint — not on large fills.
  • Lists aren't cards. History and exercise lists use rows with dividers, not a box per item.
  • Semantics stay separate. PB amber, success green, delete red keep their meaning in every pillar.
  • The Coach carries its lavender everywhere — Home pick, chat bubbles, in-workout notes, summary — so its voice is recognisable anywhere.
