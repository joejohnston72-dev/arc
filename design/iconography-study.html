Iconography study

Three directions to replace the stock Lucide set, plus five app-mark options. Same 24px grid, round caps and joins, currentColor throughout, so any of them drops into shared/icons.js without touching call sites.

Stroke
Context
NOWLucide, 2px

Generic open-source set. Heavy against Google Sans at small sizes; shared with thousands of apps.

ARefined line

Custom geometric line set. Subtle arc DNA: arched door, speech corner, bowl, dial. Reads as standard icons.

BDuotone

Set A plus a soft fill layer in the pillar hue. Depth without glow; fill can carry the active state.

CARC signature

Pronounced motif: enclosed forms are open arcs with a gap at top-right and a spark dot in the gap. C+ adds the duotone fill.

Glyph matrix

Same 18 concepts in every direction. Pillar hue drives the duotone fill. "Now" stays at 2px for reference; the stroke control changes A, B and C.

Tab bar

Tap a tab to move the active state. Two state models for the duotone directions: fill only when active, or a faint fill always with full tint when active.

In context

Real app surfaces at real sizes (16–20px). Switch direction with Context above. The trophy and flame glyphs are candidates to retire the 🏆 / 🔥 emoji.

App mark

Mist gradient on the graphite tile, per BRANDING.md §4. Shown at 140, 60 and 29px (home screen, Spotlight). Current dumbbell kept for reference.

To decide

  1. Direction: A, B, C or C+ (or a mix, e.g. C glyphs for nav and brand moments, A for utility actions)
  2. Stroke: 1.5, 1.75 or 2
  3. Active state: colour only, fill on active, or faint-always
  4. Replace 🏆 / 🔥 emoji with glyphs: yes / no
  5. App mark: pick one or two to refine
